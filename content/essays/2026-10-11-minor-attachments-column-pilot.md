This series is about things that other people didn’t bother getting attached to.

If I had to choose between my myriad flaws and elaborate on one of them, it would probably be my hapless sentimentality. As Oscar Wilde writes, I wish to have the luxury of an emotion without paying for it. Truth is, I believe I live quite a plaid life, not littered with heightened, gut-wrenching emotions but minor sways toward all directions depending on the day. But I still act like I do, weep over a minor inconvenience, document any pleasant surprise as euphoria, and tether myself to my casual friends as if we were soulmates holding onto nothing but each other in the tsunami, the flash flood that is life. With that comes many attachments that one really shouldn't be attached to. And this column is meant to document some of those.



As I wonder in the absurdity of the things we attach ourselves to and find solace in.
